# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith; critique ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic and relevant to PwDone.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's files, passwords, license keys or
  personal data.
- Disclose affiliation when promoting a product or service.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or discrimination.
- Hate speech, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, unsolicited advertising, or repeatedly posting the same link.
- Requests or instructions for accessing files you do not own.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to this repository and to project spaces such as issues,
pull requests, discussions and release comments. It also applies when you
represent the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to **support@pwdone.com**. Include
links, screenshots and any relevant context. Reports are reviewed promptly and
confidentially.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning,
temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces. Maintainers who do
not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
