## [0.1.0]

### Added

- First public release.
- Local password recovery for ZIP, RAR, 7z, PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.
- Guided workflow: format detection, hash extraction, validation and recovery.
- Hashcat (GPU) and John the Ripper (CPU) integration.
- Local recovery history with a clear action.
- Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and macOS 14+ (Apple Silicon) builds.
